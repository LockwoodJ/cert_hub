Universal Power Tool Rules

  • Inspect the tool before use.
  • Secure the workpiece.
  • Keep hands clear of the tool path.
  • Wait for stop or cooldown before storing.

Quick FAQ

Can I use the tool after reading this?

Only if you have completed the required certification and have instructor approval when required.

What should I do if something goes wrong?

Stop immediately, make the area safe, and ask for help before restarting.

What if my project fails?

Document what happened, identify the cause, and use the failure to improve your next attempt.
