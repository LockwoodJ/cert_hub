What students will do.

This course emphasizes hands-on engineering habits: define the problem, document decisions, prototype solutions, test results, and improve the design.

Course Focus

  • Design process and problem definition
  • Technical sketching and visual communication
  • Measurement, precision, and constraints
  • CAD modeling and engineering drawings
  • Engineering notebook documentation
  • Prototype testing and redesign

Student Expectations

  • Maintain a complete engineering notebook.
  • Follow all FabLab safety procedures.
  • Use evidence from testing to justify design decisions.
  • Submit final deliverables on time and in the required format.
  • Respect tools, materials, classmates, and the workspace.
